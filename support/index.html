Two ways to reach us

Report a problem

From the beta (fastest)

Take a screenshot while TONOS is open, tap it, then tap Share Beta Feedback. TestFlight attaches the version and device for you. If TONOS crashed, TestFlight offers to send the report the next time you open it.

By email

CONTACT_EMAIL_TBD@example.com. Include the version from TONOS → Settings → About (long-press to copy it). Please don't attach exports of your health data — a screenshot is enough.

First-run questions

Common answers

Tiles are empty or say they need more data.
TONOS learns from what Apple Health already holds. Readiness needs a few nights of sleep and heart-rate variability from an Apple Watch; training load needs workouts with heart rate. Without a Watch, gym, body and check-in still work and the rest fills in as data arrives.
I said no to a Health permission. How do I change it?
Settings app → Health → Data Access & Devices → TONOS. Turn on the types you want, then pull down on Today to refresh.
Moving to a new iPhone?
In TONOS: Settings → Your data → Back up everything, and save the file to iCloud Drive. On the new phone, Restore from backup. Apple Health data moves with your iCloud or iPhone backup, not with this file.
Is this medical advice?
No. TONOS gives training guidance. If a metric looks wrong for days — a resting heart rate that keeps climbing, for instance — see a professional.

Your data

TONOS has no servers and no accounts, so support never sees your health data unless you send it. How the app handles data is on the privacy page.
