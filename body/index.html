Fuel

Refueling that matches the session

The glucose disposal window

After a hard session, a capacity curve models how fast glycogen actually refills based on that workout's type and intensity — with a live countdown for the prime carb window and the wider insulin-sensitive window, so you know not just what to eat but when it matters most.

A plan, not just a number

Carb recommendations split by glycemic index (fast for immediate refuel, mixed for steady release), an intra-workout fueling planner for long efforts, and an 8-week metabolic progression plan that ramps volume with built-in deload weeks.
