Load management

Is your training load in the sweet spot?

Fitness · Fatigue · Form

42-day Fitness (CTL) and 7-day Fatigue (ATL) plotted together — their difference is Form, which tells you whether you're fresh and race-ready or productively overloaded.

Status reads as one of: fresh & tapered, race-ready balance, productive overload, or over-reached.

Monotony & strain

High day-to-day sameness (monotony ≥ 2.0) combined with high weekly load is the classic overtraining precursor — the app flags it and suggests mixing in genuinely easy and genuinely hard days rather than a week of medium efforts.

Week over week

Consistency, made visible

Cardio, intensity and strength minutes combine into a Weekly Charge — supercharged weeks (≥80%) spark, and a Peak Week (every target hit) earns a crown. A streak counter tracks consecutive weeks at ≥50%, so long strings of consistency are visible at a glance.

Running Coach

Built for runners

Race prediction

Estimated 5K, 10K, half and full marathon times from a real recent hard effort — never extrapolated from VO₂max alone, which over-predicts for heavier athletes.

Aerobic economy

Heart rate at a fixed easy pace, or pace at a fixed easy heart rate — tracked over time as the clearest read on aerobic fitness improving.

Decoupling

First-half vs. second-half pace-to-heart-rate drift on longer runs — a rising ratio flags fatigue or pacing/fueling issues mid-run.

Running dynamics

Cadence, ground contact time and vertical oscillation from your Apple Watch, tracked as a trend rather than a one-off number.

Cycling Coach

Built for cyclists

Power profile & phenotype

4DP
sprint · anaerobic · VO2max · threshold

A mean-maximal power curve across four durations classifies you as a Sprinter, Pursuer, Climber or Time-Trialist — with concrete training suggestions for your shape.

Climb detection

Sustained gradients are detected automatically from ride GPS — gradient, duration, elevation gained, and VAM (vertical ascent rate) for longer efforts.

Ride load

TSS
NP · IF · zone distribution

Normalized Power, Intensity Factor and Training Stress Score from your power data, with time-in-zone across every recent ride.

Pedalling consistency

Cadence and speed tracked together — when they decouple mid-ride (speed drops while cadence holds), that's fatigue showing.
