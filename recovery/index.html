The centerpiece

Daily Charge

Four pillars — Readiness, Sleep, Exertion and Energy — combine into one 0–100% score that fills through the day as each one lands. Cross 80% and it goes supercharged; hit every single target and it's a peak day.

On a rest day, the pillars swap to Restraint and Recovery rebound — so a well-executed easy day can supercharge too, instead of reading as a failed one.

A pillar with no data (say, the watch wasn't worn overnight) is withheld from the score, not counted as a zero — a tracking gap is never mistaken for a bad day.

Sleep architecture

Deep, REM, Core and Awake time against target windows, with a night-by-night hypnogram of stage transitions.

Rest Reserve

A rolling 7-night debt tracker against your own seeded nightly aim, with concrete “add ~X h over Y nights” guidance.

Illness detection

When resting HR, wrist temperature and breathing rate all drift up together — the classic early-infection pattern — the app flags recovery mode, mutes the day's training push (rest still counts, and streaks are protected), and suggests seeing a professional if it persists. This is training guidance, not a diagnosis.

Rest days, handled properly

When readiness is low or training load has run hot, the app suggests rest — and swaps the day's tiles to Move (an easy walk), Daylight, and Unwind, each with its own target. A manual Charge/Rest toggle always lets you override the call.
