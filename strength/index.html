A deload that suggests itself

When readiness drops below 65 and you haven't logged a set yet today, the app proposes a deload — progressively lighter loads as readiness falls further, capped at 30% off, with reps trimmed rather than sets dropped, so the movement pattern stays intact. One tap pre-fills the session; every exercise can still be overridden by hand.

Every session

Logging that thinks with you

Autoregulated sets

Load, reps and RPE (rate of perceived exertion) per set — pre-filled lighter automatically on a low-readiness day.

Double progression

Hit the top of your rep range? The app suggests adding a rep next session, or adding load once you've maxed reps — protecting technique over chasing numbers.

Personal records, tracked forever

Lifetime PRs per exercise with date and load, plus a 30-day e1RM trend chart across every logged session.

Retire or replace, keep the history

Swapping an exercise out doesn't erase its progress — retired lifts stay visible, and a replacement carries the training focus forward.
