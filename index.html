No servers

Every score, chart and poster is computed on your iPhone. There's no backend to send data to.

No accounts

No sign-up, no login, no profile stored anywhere but your own device.

No analytics SDKs

Nothing tracks usage, and no third party ever sees your health data — not even in aggregate.

The craft

Every formula is independently unit-tested, on-device.

The analytics core is pure, dependency-free logic — no UI, no networking — tested apart from the app itself. Features ship in disciplined, reviewed phases rather than all at once.
